Why your own voice

Affirmations read by someone else are easy to tune out. Your own voice is harder to dismiss — you already believe the person saying it. SpeakTrue is built around that one idea and nothing else.

Say it out loud

Write your own affirmation or tap one of the suggestions. Then press record and speak it. Most takes are under ten seconds.

  • Twelve categories, from Confidence to Calm to Focus
  • Record again as many times as you like before saving
  • Nothing to set up, no account to create

1,200 affirmations to start from

A blank page is hard. Browse the full library, search it, filter by category, and record over any line that sounds like you.

  • Search and filter by category or by what you've recorded
  • Heart the ones you keep coming back to
  • Edit the wording so it's yours, not ours

Come back to it

Each day brings up one of your affirmations to play. A reminder can nudge you at a time you pick, and a streak counts the days you show up.

  • Play any recording back in a tap
  • Set the reminder for whatever time actually suits you
  • Your streak is the only number the app keeps score of

Your voice stays on your phone

Recordings are saved on your device and never uploaded. There's no account, no sign-in, and nothing to cancel.

  • Free to download and try
  • One payment unlocks unlimited recordings, forever
  • No subscription, no ads, no tracking
